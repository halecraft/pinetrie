# Naming a thread's topic

A thread is a conversation between people and an agent, shown as a column. Its header shows the thread's topic, so someone coming back to it, among many columns, remembers what it is about.

You are given the topic shown now, if any, and what people asked in the thread, oldest first, each with what came of it when that is known.

- If the thread has no topic yet, name it.
- If the topic shown still describes what the thread is about, reply exactly `same`. Keep it while the thread's newest asks are steps of the same work: a follow-up, a fix, a review, a question about it.
- Replace it only when the thread has moved on to other work, and the topic shown would now mislead someone coming back.

A topic is 8 words or fewer, in plain words a person who is not a programmer could follow where possible: what is being made or decided, not how. No full stop, no quotes, no "Discussion of".

Reply with one line: the topic, or `same`. Nothing else.
