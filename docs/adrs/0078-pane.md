# ADR-0078 — A column's pane: one place to open what you read, beside the thread or over a narrow screen

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Area:** `docs/06-interface.md`, `packages/web/TECHNICAL.md`
- **Evidence:** S66
- **Amends:**
  - ADR-0047: the review is one of the column's pane's subjects, which owns
    its width, head, Escape and focus, and covers a narrow screen.
- **Amended by:**
  - ADR-0087: the pane's width and the narrow line come from the strip's free
    width, not the window's, which no longer fits since your list took the
    left edge (ADR-0085).
  - ADR-0182: the pane puts the image it shows on the canvas.

Replaces the image viewer over the page (`packages/client/blob-image.tsx`,
S66).

**Context.** A column is 28rem (ADR-0047). Some of what a thread shows needs
more:
- **Tables.** A markdown table had to fit 28rem. Message text breaks
  anywhere (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`), and the table took that into account
  when it sized its columns, so it split words ("Wo/rki/ng") instead of
  scrolling. A table now breaks only where a word can't fit on a line of its
  own, so words stay whole. A table wider than the column scrolls sideways
  in it.
- **Images.** A click on a stored image showed it over the page in a modal
  `<dialog>`, which a click or Escape closes. You can't read it while you
  write a reply.
- **Charts**, once rendered, will need the same room.

The column already has a place for wide things. The offer's review, or an
ask's, opens on the thread's right and keeps the thread and composer in view
(ADR-0047). It was the only thing that could open there, and two pieces of
`Column` state (`reviewing`, `askReview`) kept the two reviews from being
open at once. Nothing in pine·trie has a layout for narrow screens
(`docs/06-interface.md` defers them). A 28rem column is already wider than
most phones.

**Decision.**
- **A column has one pane, and it holds one thing at a time:** the offer's
  review, an ask's review, a table, a stored image, and later a chart. The
  column keeps that as one value whose kinds are those subjects, so two
  can't be open at once. Opening another replaces it. Opening the same one
  again, the pane's close control, or Escape with focus in the pane closes
  it. While a table or image is in the pane, its source in the thread is
  outlined in the column's colour.
- **On a wide screen the pane is beside the thread**, where the review is
  now, at the review's width. **On a narrow screen it covers the page**, as
  a modal `<dialog>` in the top layer, with a back control. Closing it
  gives focus back to what opened it. "Narrow" means the window can't hold
  the thread beside the pane at the pane's narrowest. The same subject
  draws in both, so a review gets a narrow-screen layout too.
- **Every table in a message has a bar of its own actions, always shown**,
  above its right edge. The first is **⤢**, which opens the table in the
  pane and is pressed while it is there. Copying is the expected next
  action. The bar is on every table, not only one that overflows: a table
  may need its actions even when it fits, and on a touch screen there is no
  hover to reveal them. A reply still being written has no entry yet, so
  its tables show the bar with ⤢ unavailable until the reply is committed.
- **The pane names a table; it holds no copy.** A table is named by its
  message's entry and its position among that message's tables, and the
  pane draws it from the message. There it scrolls both ways and keeps its
  header row in view. The pane closes when the column stops drawing that
  message.
- **A click on a stored image opens it in the pane** (one the host keeps,
  ADR-0042, in a message's markdown or a tool's row, ADR-0051). It fits the
  pane's width. A click on it switches to its own size, with the pane
  scrolling, and back. The pane's header links to the image in a new tab,
  and a Cmd- or Ctrl-click on the image still opens it there.
- **What the pane shows is never stored,** as the review was not: it is
  kept while the column is mounted. It closes when its subject goes. For
  the review, that is when the offer is taken or withdrawn, as before.

**Consequences.**
- One way to open what you read, on every screen. The image viewer, its
  styles, and its note in `packages/client/TECHNICAL.md` go. S66 still
  applies: a narrow screen's pane is a modal `<dialog>`.
- A table is one line taller, for its bar.
- On a wide screen, a large image is smaller in the pane (40–64rem) than it
  was over the page (95% of the window). A new tab shows it whole.
- A column with its pane open is wider, and can push the columns right of
  it past the strip's edge, as a review does now (ADR-0047).
- Each column can have a pane open, so two columns can show two tables side
  by side. Two tables from one column can't.
- A window resized across the narrow line while the pane is open moves the
  pane between its two places, and it starts again at its top.

**Alternatives rejected.**
- **A wide column**, a header toggle that draws the thread at twice its
  width, stored like the thread's details (ADR-0073). It widens every
  message for the sake of one table or image, at about 120 characters a
  line of prose. It needed a rule for when it meets a review (draw at one
  width while reviewing, keep the choice for after) and is meaningless on a
  phone, where one width is already too wide.
- **Keeping the image viewer beside the pane.** It would be two ways to
  open things, chosen by kind. The viewer hides the thread and composer,
  and closes at a click.
- **A table growing in place, over its neighbours.** The column's body is a
  scroll container, so it cuts off anything wider than the column.
- **A popover.** It closes on a scroll outside it (`popover.tsx`).
- **A `position: fixed` overlay on a narrow screen.** It would cover only
  what shares its stacking context, and a transform on any ancestor makes
  that ancestor its frame. The top layer is above everything, and a modal
  dialog makes the page behind it inert and handles Escape and focus.
- **⤢ only on a table that overflows.** It would come and go as the window
  or the pane changes width, and copying applies to a table that fits.
- **The bar only on hover.** A touch screen has no hover, and hidden actions
  are found late.
- **A copy of the table in the pane.** The column would hold a tree to
  compare on every render, and could not tell when its message leaves the
  column.
- **Storing what the pane shows,** as the thread's details are stored. You
  open a table or an image to look at it for a while, and a reload should
  not bring it back.
- **A pane for each kind**, or several open at once. That brings back the
  state that kept the two reviews from both opening, and is wider than
  the strip.
