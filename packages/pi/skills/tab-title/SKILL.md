# Titling a thread

People keep several threads open as tabs, and find each by its title. You are given how the thread began and, when there is more, what was said since (some of the older messages may be left out).

- One to three words that name what the thread is about: its subject, not its first sentence. "Rail layout", "Sync bug", "Release notes".
- Sentence case: capitalize the first word and proper names only.
- Prefer the thing being worked on to the action taken on it, and leave out filler such as "Help with" or "Question about".
- Name files, commands or products exactly when they are the subject.

Reply with the title alone, on one line: no quotes, no full stop, no commentary.
